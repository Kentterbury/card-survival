# Third-party assets

All 3D models and animations in this folder come from KayKit by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Attribution is not required but appreciated.

- KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 — https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
- KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons 1.0 — https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Skeletons-1.0
- KayKit Dungeon Remastered 1.0 — https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0

Files were re-exported with `tools/optimize-assets.mjs` (unused animations removed, meshopt compression).

## Forest beasts (`creatures/`)

Wolf (Ultimate Animated Animal Pack) and GreenBlob / GreenSpikyBlob / PinkBlob / Pigeon (Ultimate Monsters) by
Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0. Re-exported with `tools/optimize-creatures.mjs`
(unused animations removed, meshopt compression).

## Spell card icons

The spell card pictures (`src/ui/spellArt.ts`, generated by `tools/build-spell-art.mjs`) are icons from
[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) by Lorc, Delapouite and contributors, licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). They are recoloured with each spell's element gradient.

## Sound effects (`sfx/`) and music (`music/`)

All released under CC0 1.0 (public domain); picked and converted to MP3 with `tools/build-sfx.mjs`.

- Kenney (https://kenney.nl): RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, Music Jingles, Casino Audio.
- OpenGameArt.org sound effects: "80 CC0 RPG SFX" (rubberduck), "RPG Sound Pack" (artisticdude),
  "Ice spells" (bart), "Freeze spell" (artisticdude), "Cure magic" (Someoneman), "Magic Spell SFX" (JaggedStone),
  "3 Dark Magic Spells" (qubodup), "Fireball" (Julien Matthey).
- OpenGameArt.org music: "Medieval: The Bard's Tale" (RandomMind, title), "Dark Forest Theme" (cynicmusic, chapter 1),
  "Dungeon Theme" (Katana, chapter 2), "Going Deep" (section31, chapter 3), "Epic Boss Battle" (Juhani Junkala, final battle).
